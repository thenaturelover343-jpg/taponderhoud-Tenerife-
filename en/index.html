Tap service for businesses in South Tenerife

We handle line cleaning, full maintenance, beer tap repair and installation of beer systems.

A dirty installation shows fast: beer loses freshness, foam changes and service behind the bar slows down. That is why we check the lines, taps, visible connections, pressure and CO2 with a clear routine.

  • Professional cleaning of beer lines and taps
  • CO2, pressure, connection and leak checks
  • Urgent service and periodic maintenance
  • Service in Spanish, Dutch and English

Service for bars, hotels and events

We work for bars, restaurants, hotels, beach clubs, cafes, terraces and events in South Tenerife. Every installation has its own rhythm: a small bar does not need the same follow-up as a hotel with several serving points.

During a normal visit we clean the beer lines, check the taps and look for leaks, pressure loss and loose connections. If the venue already has a maintenance routine, we follow it. If not, we suggest a simple frequency based on real bar use.

We also help with faults when a line pours badly, creates too much foam, runs slowly or loses pressure. In those cases we check the basics first: keg, gas, regulator, connections and tap.

The main service area is South Tenerife: Adeje, Costa Adeje, Playa de las Americas, Los Cristianos, San Miguel de Abona and Las Chafiras. Other areas can be checked on request.
